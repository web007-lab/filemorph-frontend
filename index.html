Accurate Target KB Image Compression Tool

Welcome to FileMorph, the most advanced online utility designed specifically for students, job applicants, and professionals. Most standard image converters reduce resolution linearly, which leads to unpredictable file sizes and blurry images. FileMorph utilizes a multi-pass binary search algorithm that balances pixel dimensions and quantization tables to lock down file sizes exactly below your target constraint (1KB to 300KB) without unnecessary visual degradation.

How to Compress Images to Exact Size in 3 Steps?

Step 1: Upload your Image. Click on the 'Browse File' button or drag and drop your JPG, PNG, or WebP file into the drop zone.

Step 2: Enter Target Size. In the input box, type your exact required size (e.g., if the exam form says 'Under 50KB', type 50). You can choose any value between 1 KB and 300 KB.

Step 3: Compress & Download. Click the 'Compress Image' button. Our engine will calculate the best resolution and quality balance. Once done, the optimized file will automatically download to your device.
